About us

Welcome to Tarak Tube Co.

Tarak Tube Co., a company based in Burabazar, Kolkata, West Bengal, India, that was established in the year 1993. The company is a partnership-based firm and has been engaged in the business of wholesaling, retailing and supplying various products.

Our primary focus is on the supply of HDPE, PVC Borewell, UPVC, CPVC, SWR Pipes & Fittings, Ribbed Strainer Filters, India MII Hand Pump, MII Cylinders and several other products. Apart from these products, we also offer a wide range of other products, such as CI Bib Cocks, Stop Cocks, Brass Ferrules, GI pipes & Fittings, PVC Garden Pipes, CP fittings and the list goes on.
